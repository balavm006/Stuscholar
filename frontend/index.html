Rules-as-data eligibility

Every scheme declares its own rules in JSON. Adding a new scheme needs zero code — just one entry plus its conflict edges.

🧮

Exact conflict-aware optimizer

Schemes that can't be claimed together form graph edges. The engine returns the maximum-value conflict-free combination — provably optimal.
